Reference Style: Clean, soft-shadow, modern minimal. Core Vibe: "Organic Trust" – sophisticated and muted, not neon/cluttered.

1. Visual Identity & Color Palette

Canvas: #F7F6F6 (Soft Cloud) - Reduces eye strain.

Primary: #4C5273 (Steel Indigo) - Buttons, headers.

Text (Dark): #252837 (Gunmetal) - Headings, prices.

Secondary: #686E8F (Slate Blue) - Subtitles.

Accent: #A3584E (Terracotta) - Market price strikethroughs, discount badges.

Border: #ACACBF (Muted Silver).

2. Typography

Font: Plus Jakarta Sans or Inter (Geometric sans-serif).

Scale: H1 Headlines (24px Bold), Product Names (18px Semi-Bold), Price Tag (20px Bold).

3. UI Component Library

A. The "Price Comparison" Card

White card on soft grey background.

Layout: Top 60% Image, Bottom 40% Pricing.

Price Block: Large dark price (e.g., ₹18) vs. Small terracotta strikethrough (e.g., ~~Market: ₹30~~).

Action: Circular "Add" button floating on the image.

B. "Flash Price" Widget

Gradient background (#4C5273 to #252837).

Vegetable image breaks container bounds (3D effect).

Countdown timer pill in Terracotta.

C. Cart & Navigation

Nav: Bottom bar, no hamburger menus.

Cart: Slide-up bottom sheet with blurred background. "Slide to Pay" interaction.

4. Screen Layouts

Home: Sticky header, horizontal "Flash Price" scroll, masonry grid for products.

Product Detail: Half-screen image, "Harvested at [Time]" tag, Price comparison bar chart.

5. Implementation Notes

Use .svg icons and .webp images for speed.

Use Flutter Hero widgets for smooth image transitions.
